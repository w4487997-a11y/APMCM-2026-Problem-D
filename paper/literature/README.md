# Q1 Literature Research

This directory is the single literature source of truth for the paper-writing workflow.

## Reading order for the next Codex chat
1. literature_catalog.md — verified bibliography and candidate disambiguation.
2. literature_comparison.md — cross-paper comparison.
3. q1_academic_positioning.md — evidence-bounded positioning of the actual Q1 v1.
4. references.bib — verified BibTeX entries.
5. individual_papers/ — individual reading notes.

## Status convention
- VERIFIED-METADATA: bibliographic identity confirmed on publisher/official source.
- ABSTRACT-REVIEWED: publisher abstract and metadata reviewed; not a full-text review.
- FULLTEXT-REVIEWED: full article body reviewed.
- TODO-FULLTEXT: full paper or supplementary material still needs detailed reading.

Do not treat an abstract-level note as a completed full-paper review. Do not upload copyrighted publisher PDFs unless the license explicitly permits redistribution. Save DOI, publisher links, notes and lawful open-access materials instead.

## Scope and data protection
The present Q1 model is the repository's actual hgb_gap_mix composition-only v1. Literature is used for scientific context and method comparison, not to infer hidden competition labels. Never query external material databases to identify or infer the 19 hidden V=0 cases in pool 04.
