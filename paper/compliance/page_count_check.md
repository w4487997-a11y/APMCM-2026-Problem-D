# Page Count Check

L1 verifies that every generated page is A4 and that Summary Sheet, contents, and body begin in the required order. The structural manuscript is not a final page-count acceptance.

- Summary Sheet: expected page 1.
- Contents: expected page 2.
- Main body: page counter restarts at 1 after the contents.
- Final main-body maximum: 25 pages, to be checked after content freeze.
- Appendix: count and release treatment require final manual confirmation against the official specification.
