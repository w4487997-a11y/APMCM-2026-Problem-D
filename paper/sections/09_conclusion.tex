\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

On file~03, which informed model selection, the Q1 surrogate achieved
MAE $2.4715$, Spearman correlation $0.9383$, and 20 true Top-50 hits.  A
retrospective budget-50 replay retrieved 23 records with $V<5$, compared
with a uniform-sampling expectation of $0.8818$.  The low-tail MAE of
$8.2654$ and the absence of labels for file~04 limit the interpretation
to candidate prioritization.  For Q2, the 104-variable mapping selected
Y--Ti--NO2 ($\widehat V_{23}=1.491928$); for Q3, the groupwise mappings
selected Na--Nb--O3 ($\widehat V_{23}=0.540023$).  The Q2/Q3 pools are
unlabeled, so their prediction errors cannot be estimated independently.

The adopted eight-bit encoding preserves each previously identified group
winner and separates it from illegal states, but it is oracle-assisted and
does not preserve the ordering among non-winners.  Uniform quantization
created tied minima, while the 107-variable projection changed the
optimum.  The study therefore demonstrates an auditable encoded-proxy
screening workflow, not physical validation of the selected materials
or quantum advantage.
