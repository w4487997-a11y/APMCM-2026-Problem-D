\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

On selection-involved file~03, Q1 achieved MAE $2.4715$, Spearman $0.9383$,
and 20 Top-50 hits.  Its budget-50 replay found 23 records with $V<5$ versus
a random expectation of $0.8818$; low-tail MAE $8.2654$ and unlabeled file~04
restrict the result to prioritization.  The 104-variable mappings selected
Y--Ti--NO2 for Q2 ($\widehat V_{23}=1.491928$) and Na--Nb--O3 for Q3
($\widehat V_{23}=0.540023$), but the unlabeled pools give no independent
error estimate.

The eight-bit mapping separates its known winner from illegal states but is
oracle-assisted and discards other ordering; uniform quantization tied minima,
and the 107-variable projection changed the optimum.  The outcome remains
auditable encoded-proxy screening, not physical validation or quantum advantage.
