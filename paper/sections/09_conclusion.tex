\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The Q1 surrogate achieved MAE $2.4715$, RMSE $4.5827$, Spearman $0.9383$,
and 20 true Top-50 hits on the selection-involved file~03 assessment.  Its
budget-50 replay retrieved 23 records with $V<5$, versus a uniform expectation
of $0.8818$, supporting candidate prioritization but not independent physical
validation.

For Q2 and Q3, frozen proxy enumeration and the adopted 104-variable QUBOs
selected Y--Ti--$\mathrm{NO}_2$ ($\widehat V_{23}=1.491928$) and
Na--Nb--$\mathrm O_3$ ($0.540023$).  These oracle-assisted matrices preserve
known group minima within the recorded coefficient range but discard
non-winner ordering.  Qualified blind-pool runs withheld optimum identities
while using complete within-pool score ranges; their quantized tiers exposed
11--34 ties.  The 107-variable projection changed the optimum, and three of
four small SPQC tasks returned a group proxy argmin.  Together, these results
separate surrogate, mapping, quantization, CPU-sampling, and hardware evidence
without claiming physical optimality, repeatable hardware performance, or
quantum advantage.
