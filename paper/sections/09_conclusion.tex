\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The Q1 surrogate achieved MAE $2.4715$, RMSE $4.5827$, Spearman
correlation $0.9383$, and 20 true Top-50 hits on file~03.  Its
budget-50 retrospective replay retrieved 23 records with $V<5$,
compared with a uniform-sampling expectation of $0.8818$.  Because
file~03 informed model selection, these figures are selection-involved
assessments rather than independent external-validation results.  The
low-tail MAE of $8.2654$ and the absence of labels for file~04 further
limit the Q1 claim to candidate prioritization.

Exhaustive enumeration of the frozen Q2/Q3 proxy tables identified
Y--Ti--NO2 ($\widehat V_{23}=1.491928$) and Na--Nb--O3
($\widehat V_{23}=0.540023$) as numerical minima within their supplied
candidate domains.  Their margins to the runner-up candidates are
$0.313819$ and $0.013123$.  The Q2/Q3 pools are unlabeled, so their
prediction errors cannot be estimated independently.  The file~03
low-tail MAE is much larger than either margin, but it comes from a
different model fit and is not an uncertainty bound for
$\widehat V_{23}$.  We therefore do not interpret either proxy minimum
as a statistically resolved or physically unique material winner.

For the full pools, the adopted two-level eight-bit QUBOs preserve the
already enumerated group minima and separate them from illegal states.
Because their construction uses the known group minima, these matrices
are oracle-assisted representation and sampling tests, not independent
discovery methods; they also discard the ordering among non-winning
candidates.  The complementary blind-pool experiments use within-pool
score scaling without supplying winner identities.  Local simulated
annealing found the floating-point blind-pool minima in 25--27 of 30
batches, while the eight-bit models hit in 11--16 of 30 batches and
introduced 11--34 minimum-tier ties.  These are oracle-free searches
over unlabeled blind-pool proxy objectives, not physical validation or
Kaiwu/SPQC evidence.

The study's contribution is therefore an auditable comparison of
oracle-assisted full-pool encoding and oracle-free blind-pool search,
together with a composition-based screening workflow and separately
defined mapping, quantization, and sampling checks.  It does not
establish physical feasibility, a statistically unique material winner,
repeatable hardware performance, or quantum advantage.
