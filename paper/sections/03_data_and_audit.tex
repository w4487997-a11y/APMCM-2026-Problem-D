\section{Data and Audit}
\label{sec:data-audit}

\subsection{Data universe and integrity}

The organizer's data cover the complete $52\times52\times7$ factorial
grid of 18,928 ordered compositions \cite{apmcm2026problemD}.  Files~02
(11,256 rows; 1,608 cation-pair groups) and~03 (3,969 rows; 567 groups)
contain the labeled development and validation records.  File~04 holds
the remaining 3,703 unlabeled rows (529 groups).  The candidate pools are
file~06 (15,225 rows), Q2 file~07 (2,704 rows), Q3 file~08 (8,112 rows),
and the blind-pair subsets in files~11 (529 rows) and~12 (1,587 rows).
Those blind pairs form a sparse subset of the $52\times52$ cation grid,
not a $23\times23$ Cartesian product.  File~12 repeats the same 529
ordered pairs for each of three anion groups.  Files~11 and~12 contain no
physical labels.

We read the CSV files as UTF-8, allowing an optional byte-order mark,
and used the ordered triple $(A,B,X)$ as the composition key.  The audit
preserved source order and text, then checked all nine official SHA-256
values, schemas, row counts, duplicate keys, pool intersections, finite
labels, and nonnegative penalties.  No rows were deleted, imputed, or
supplemented with external data.  Recomputed $V$ differed from the supplied
labels by at most $1.019\times10^{-5}$ in each labeled file.  This is below
the conservative rounding bounds of $3.082\times10^{-5}$ for file~02 and
$2.890\times10^{-5}$ for file~03.  The maximum
$|\mathrm{volume}-a_{\rm lat}^3|$ was $4.20\times10^{-5}$ and
$4.35\times10^{-5}$~\AA$^3$, respectively.  These six-decimal differences
do not warrant changing Equation~\eqref{eq:formulation-V}.  The problem
statement reports 19 zero-violation and 15 strictly feasible records in
the full dataset.  Neither public labeled split contains such a record,
and we did not infer their identities.

\subsection{Distribution and screening difficulty}

The label distribution is strongly concentrated at zero band gap.
This occurs in 10,824 file~02 rows ($96.16\%$) and 3,828 file~03 rows
($96.45\%$); each such row receives a gap penalty of
$\phi(1.4)=7.84$.  By contrast, only 222 training rows ($1.97\%$) and
70 validation rows ($1.76\%$) have $V<5$; the minimum scores are 0.0004
and 0.0196, respectively.  Positive gaps occur in 432 training rows and
141 validation rows, of which 139 and 40 fall within
$1.4\le E_g\le3.1$.  Within the observed $V<5$ subsets, formation-energy
and CBM penalties account for 80.7\% and 77.5\% of the total penalty in
files~02 and~03.  These observations are consistent with the difficulty of predicting
the low-$V$ tail using a model optimized mainly for the majority regime.
They motivate the mixture model in Section~\ref{sec:q1-surrogate}, but do
not identify a causal mechanism.

\subsection{Training protocols and leakage controls}

For Q1, all seven anion variants of an ordered $(A,B)$ pair stay in the
same fold.  We split the 1,608 file~02 groups deterministically into five
folds (seed 42), with 321 or 322 validation groups per fold.  Preprocessing
is refitted using each fold's training partition.  File~03 is used for the frozen model-selection comparison and is
therefore not an independent external test set.  Its reported performance
and subsequent budget replay are selection-involved retrospective
assessments.  The released \texttt{q1\_final} model is then fitted on
file~02 alone, and its file~04 predictions are treated as blind outputs
rather than validation results.

Q2/Q3 instead require candidate scores.  Their \texttt{q23\_final} reuses
the frozen architecture but fits 02+03, then scores files~07, 08, 11, and 12
without physical labels.  Its full-pool 07/08 scores feed the reported QUBOs;
03 is never reused as unseen evidence.  Files~11/12 were also used in a
separate blind-pool audit: four tasks (Q2--NO2 and Q3--O3/NO2/FO2) were each
encoded in floating-point and integer form, producing eight dedicated
104-variable matrices over the sparse legal mask.  Each model was solved by
local simulated annealing with 300 samples.  This is independent matrix
construction on the blind pools, not post-hoc filtering of the full-pool
solution, and it is neither a Kaiwu SDK solve nor an SPQC-550 run.  The
affine score range is computed from each blind prediction table itself; no
enumerated winner identity is supplied to the encoding, and each matrix is
frozen before sampling.  The procedure is an oracle-free search over the
blind-pool proxy objective, not a physical-label validation, because
files~11/12 remain unlabeled.

Model inputs contain only $A$, $B$, $X$, and deterministic composition
features.  We exclude identifiers, row positions, pool labels, and target
values.  Each encoder is fitted on the relevant training partition;
candidate order is preserved; ties are broken by numerical ID; and hashes
are recorded for the model, inputs, and outputs.  Separate Q1 and Q2/Q3
artifacts keep the 02+03 refit out of the Q1 release model.  No external
lookup or hidden-label inference is used.  These controls document the
workflow but cannot remove surrogate error.
