\section{Data and Audit}
\label{sec:data-audit}

\subsection{Data universe and integrity}

The official data form a complete $52\times52\times7$ factorial grid of
18,928 ordered compositions \cite{apmcm2026problemD}.  Labeled files~02
(11,256 rows; 1,608 cation-pair groups) and~03 (3,969; 567 groups) support
development and the Q2/Q3 refit.  File~04 contains the remaining 3,703
unlabeled rows (529 groups).  Composition pools are file~06 (15,225 rows),
Q2 file~07 (2,704), Q3 file~08 (8,112), and blind-pair subsets files~11
(529) and~12 (1,587).  The 529 blind pairs are a sparse subset of the
$52\times52$ cation grid, not a $23\times23$ Cartesian product; file~12
contains the same 529 ordered pairs for each of three anion groups.  Thus
each ordered pair contributes all seven X values to exactly one of 02--04;
files~11/12 add no physical labels.

All CSVs were read as UTF-8 with optional byte-order marks, using ordered
$(A,B,X)$ as the composition key.  The audit retained source order and text,
verified all nine official SHA-256 values, schemas, row counts, uniqueness,
pool intersections, finite labels, and nonnegative penalties.  No row was
deleted, imputed, or externally augmented.  Recomputed $V$ differed by at
most $1.019\times10^{-5}$ in both labeled files, below conservative rounding
bounds $3.082\times10^{-5}$ (02) and $2.890\times10^{-5}$ (03); the maximum
$|\mathrm{volume}-a_{\rm lat}^3|$ was $4.20\times10^{-5}$ and
$4.35\times10^{-5}$~\AA$^3$.  These six-decimal effects do not justify
changing Equation~\eqref{eq:formulation-V}.  The statement reports 19
zero-violation and 15 strictly feasible records in the complete data, but
neither public labeled split contains one; their identities were not
inferred.

\subsection{Distribution and screening difficulty}

Zero band gap dominates: 10,824 file~02 rows ($96.16\%$) and 3,828 file~03
rows ($96.45\%$) have $E_g=0$, which alone contributes
$\phi(1.4)=7.84$.  Conversely, only 222 training rows ($1.97\%$) and 70
validation rows ($1.76\%$) have $V<5$, with minima 0.0004 and 0.0196.
Positive gaps occur in 432 and 141 rows, of which 139 and 40 lie in
$1.4\le E_g\le3.1$.  Within the observed $V<5$ subsets, formation-energy
and CBM penalties contribute 80.7\% and 77.5\% of total penalty in 02 and
03.  This association is consistent with the observed difficulty of low-tail
prediction; it does not identify a causal mechanism.  Majority-regime
accuracy alone can therefore miss the low tail, motivating the mixture in
Section~\ref{sec:q1-surrogate}.

\subsection{Training protocols and leakage controls}

For Q1, all seven X variants of an ordered pair remain together.  The 1,608
file~02 groups are deterministically split into five folds (seed 42; 321 or
322 validation groups), with preprocessing refitted inside each fold.
File~03 is used once for frozen selection after candidate specifications are
frozen; the released \texttt{q1\_final} then fits file~02 only.  File~04
predictions are blind outputs, not validation results.

Q2/Q3 instead require candidate scores.  Their \texttt{q23\_final} reuses
the frozen architecture but fits 02+03, then scores files~07, 08, 11, and 12
without physical labels.  Its full-pool 07/08 scores feed the reported QUBOs;
03 is never reused as unseen evidence.  Files~11/12 were also used in a
separate blind-pool audit: four tasks (Q2--NO2 and Q3--O3/NO2/FO2) were each
encoded in floating-point and integer form, producing eight dedicated
104-variable matrices over the sparse legal mask.  Each model was solved by
local simulated annealing with 300 samples.  This is independent matrix
construction on the blind pools, not post-hoc filtering of the full-pool
solution, and it is neither a Kaiwu SDK solve nor an SPQC-550 run.  The score
range used for affine scaling comes from the blind prediction table itself;
no winning candidate identity was injected, but the procedure is not a
physical-label validation because files~11/12 remain unlabeled.

Model inputs are restricted to A, B, X and deterministic composition
features.  Identifiers, positions, pool labels, and targets are excluded;
encoders fit only their training fold; candidate order is preserved; ties
use numerical ID; and model/input/output hashes are recorded.  Separate Q1
and Q2/Q3 artifacts prevent the 02+03 refit from contaminating Q1 evidence.
No external lookup or hidden-label inference is used.  These controls make
development, blind inference, and optimization traceable, although they do
not eliminate surrogate error.
